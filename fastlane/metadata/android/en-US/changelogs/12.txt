A request whose Host is written in capitals, with a trailing dot or with its default port no longer escapes the rules for its address. Regional lists are on by the device's region as well as its language, from the system's locale and time zone; the Chinese lists also cover Hong Kong, Macau and Taiwan, and anti-AD joins them. Lists that include other files are downloaded whole. $xmlprune rules are no longer applied. Several smaller faults in filtering are fixed.
