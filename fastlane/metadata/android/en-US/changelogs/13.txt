Apps that check certificates through Conscrypt directly, and servers named by an IPv6 address, work with the certificate hook. Filtering without root stops if the app selection cannot be read, and timed pauses end on time after the device sleeps. Quick Settings tile actions require unlocking the device. Root works with a plain su, and Overview no longer asks for root in VPN mode. Filtering uses less memory and CPU, the app starts faster, and several reliability faults are fixed.
