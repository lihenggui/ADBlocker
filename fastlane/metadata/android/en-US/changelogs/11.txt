Many more of AdGuard's and uBlock Origin's rules are applied: rules for single apps, rules that judge a request by what the server answers, HTML filtering, scriptlets and styles in AdGuard's syntax, redirects, cookies, removed parameters and headers, and rules by address. AdGuard's lists are taken in their Android builds and uBlock Origin's in full. Page elements are hidden unless you turn it off. The rule editor says why a rule is not applied.
