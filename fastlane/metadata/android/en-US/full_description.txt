<p>ADBlocker blocks ads and trackers in Android apps with the filter lists of browser ad blockers, such as EasyList, uBlock Origin and AdGuard. You choose the apps it filters, or let it filter all of your personal apps. ADBlocker downloads the lists you subscribe to and sends no telemetry.</p>

<p>It filters in one of two ways:</p>
<ul>
<li><b>Root mode</b> (root access, such as KernelSU, Magisk or APatch; Android 12 or later with a GKI kernel): ADBlocker sets it up with root access, without a restart. Android's VPN slot stays free for another VPN, and each connection is still attributed to the app that made it.</li>
<li><b>VPN mode</b> (no root; Android 10 or later): the filtered apps' traffic passes through ADBlocker's local VPN.</li>
</ul>

<p>With URL filtering on, ADBlocker applies URL and resource rules inside HTTPS through a local certificate authority whose key stays on the device. In root mode ADBlocker makes it available to filtered personal apps; in VPN mode you install it yourself, and only apps that trust user certificates accept it. Until then, and for any app that rejects it, HTTPS stays encrypted and only domain rules apply. You can turn URL filtering off to filter by domain only.</p>

<p>Features:</p>
<ul>
<li>Per-app choice, or all personal apps with exclusions; work profile apps with root</li>
<li>Subscriptions to EasyList, uBlock Origin and AdGuard lists, custom rules and trusted sites</li>
<li>Recording of an app's requests to see how the rules treat each one</li>
<li>Pausing for one app or for all, and a Quick Settings tile</li>
</ul>

<p>Root mode includes an LSPosed/Vector module, used only where an app checks certificates itself. Only 64-bit ARM and x86 devices are supported.</p>
