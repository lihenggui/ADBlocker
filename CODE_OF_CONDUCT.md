# ADBlocker code of conduct

## Our pledge

As ADBlocker members, contributors and maintainers, we pledge to keep
participation free of harassment. Everyone can take part regardless of
age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience, education,
socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste,
color, religion, sexual identity or sexual orientation. We welcome people
from different backgrounds and work to keep the community open to them.

## Our standards

- Treat people with kindness and consider their circumstances.
- Respect views and experiences that differ from your own.
- Give constructive feedback and receive it with courtesy.
- Own your mistakes. Apologize to the people affected and learn from what happened.
- Consider what will help the community as a whole.

These behaviors violate the code.

- Sexualized language or images, or sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insults, derogatory remarks, and personal or political attacks.
- Harassment in public or private.
- Sharing someone's private information, such as a home or email address,
  without their express permission.
- Other behavior that would reasonably be considered unprofessional.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Maintainers explain and enforce these standards. They take fair and
appropriate action when conduct is inappropriate, threatening, offensive
or harmful. They can remove, edit or reject contributions that violate
this code, including comments, commits, code, wiki edits and issues.
They explain moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Scope

This code applies in all ADBlocker community spaces and when someone
officially represents ADBlocker in public. That includes using a project
email address or social media account and representing the project in an
appointed role at an online or in-person event.

## Enforcement

Report abuse, harassment or other violations to the maintainer,
[@lihenggui on GitHub](https://github.com/lihenggui). Keep report details
out of public issues. Maintainers review and investigate reports
promptly and fairly and protect the reporter's privacy and safety.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers use these guidelines to decide how to respond to violations.

### 1. Correction

For inappropriate language or other unprofessional or unwelcome
conduct, maintainers issue a private written warning. The warning
explains what happened and why the conduct was inappropriate. Maintainers
may ask for a public apology.

### 2. Warning

A single violation or a series of violations can lead to a warning that
sets out what will happen if the conduct continues. For a stated period,
the person must avoid contact with those involved, including unsolicited
contact with the maintainers handling the case. This applies in project
spaces and elsewhere, including on social media. Breaking these terms
can lead to a temporary or permanent ban.

### 3. Temporary ban

A serious violation, including sustained misconduct, can lead to a ban
for a set period. During the ban, the person may neither interact with
the community nor communicate with it publicly. They must avoid public
and private contact with those involved, including unsolicited contact
with the maintainers handling the case. Breaking these terms can lead to
a permanent ban.

### 4. Permanent ban

A pattern of violations can lead to a permanent ban from public
participation in the community. Examples include sustained misconduct,
harassment of an individual, and aggression toward or disparagement of
a group of people.
